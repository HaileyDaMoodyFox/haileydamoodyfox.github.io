Cool Projects

This page is just a list of my favorite projects / websites, I love the more obscure side of the internet, And would love to share that with people who are interested :>

Vimm's Lair

Vimm's Lair is a website dedicated to archiving and preserving retro game roms.

Winworld

Winworld is an amazing website dedicated to archiving older versions of operating systems, Such as Windows, MacOS, And Linux.

KHI

KHI is a website dedicated to archiving video game soundtracks, In both MP3 and lossless formats.

Eden

Eden is a Nintendo Switch Emulator forked from Yuzu.

Azahar

Eden is a 3DS Emulator forked from Citra.

ShadPS4

ShadPS4 is a PS4 Emulator.

PCSX2

PCSX2 is a PS2 Emulator.
